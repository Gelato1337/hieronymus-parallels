YOUR TASK

Decide whether these two specific chapters share a real, demonstrable connection. A real connection is one of:
- a shared distinctive image, phrase, or motif specific enough that coincidence is unlikely;
- an interpretive move in 1 Enoch that develops, alludes to, or reworks a specific passage in this Isaiah chapter;
- a shared named figure, event, or eschatological scenario described in comparably specific terms.

What does NOT count:
- generic religious vocabulary shared by all ancient Jewish writing ("the Lord", "judgment", "the righteous", "sin", "glory");
- broad themes that pervade both books (divine judgment, the fate of the wicked, vindication of the righteous, cosmic upheaval). A shared theme alone is not a connection.

Most chapter pairs share nothing beyond this common vocabulary, so no connection is the normal result for most pairs — report it plainly. But when a real, specific, anchored parallel IS present, report it with equal confidence. A correct positive is exactly as valuable as a correct negative. Judge each pair on its own evidence; do not bias toward either answer.
